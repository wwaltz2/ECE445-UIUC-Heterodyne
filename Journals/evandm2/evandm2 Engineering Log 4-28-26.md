## ECE445 Engineering Log #25

April 28, 2026\
Evan McGowan

Bill put the PCB in the chassis this morning, and I came in to do final testing. Unfortunately, a day before the demo, it stopped working. Specifically, the oscillator. Cue multiple hours of troubleshooting and desoldering ICs and ripping up traces just to realize that one of the pins on the Molex connector to the oscillator potentiometer wasn't making contact. I guess it became strained after a ton of insertions and removals. I replaced the wires, and the system works now! The traces for the electrical switch were ripped up in the carnage (and frustration and desperation with ~16 hours until demo), so the digital and analog audio are now tethered together. The digital audio, of course, only plays when the button is pressed, but the analog audio seeps through.

We met with Dr. O'Keefe before I was able to fix it, and learned that our range after testing the ultrasonic calibrator with a real bat detector is extremely low in comparison. Granted, we're comparing functionality with multiple-hundred-dollar microphones and much more expensive equipment, which isn't exactly fair. At least we have a working product!!
