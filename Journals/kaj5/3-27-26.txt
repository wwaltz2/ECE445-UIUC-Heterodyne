The final PCB has been submitted, since this will be our final board I can begin to finalize the chassis design. I had to widen the case due to
the size of the PCB board, but I left the length the same. It is currently about 5 inches wide which isn't ideal as I would have preferred
the final device to be roughly smartphone sized but fitting everything is most important. I could perhaps decrease overall size by modifying
the standoffs for the screws to keep both halves of the chassis together. With the current dimensions and standoffs, there will be about 2x3 in of additional
clearance inside the chassis which should be enough to fit the battery pack. I also modified the speaker hole on the device to more appropriately
fit the dimensions of the speaker we will be using. It also looks a lot better and more like what you'd expect a speaker hole to look like compared
to the first version of the chassis. I also added a hole for the microphone and headphone jacks on the top and bottom of the chassis respectively.
Some further refinements will need to be made as we approach the end of the project. I'll need to figure out how we expect to attach the speaker
and microphone to the spots they should be in. For the speaker, I am thinking of creating a small chamber under the hole we can slide the speaker into and
glue it into place. I'll also likely need to modify the standoffs for the board inside the chassis after we figure out which screws we will be using.
Finally I need to also consider the switch we will be using for power to the device.
