I have made a more finalized chassis design. I was able to slim down the chassis to 3.5 inches (as opposed to 5) so it should be more comfortable to hold.
I did this by modifying the internal screw standoffs, I made them smaller and put them right next to the edges. I also slimmed down the internal walls slightly.
I also created some holes on the top of the chassis shell for the power switch and demo button to use. They're a bit generic but should hopefully fit whatever
switches we decide to use. If not we can always modify the case or reprint if needed. I also finalized the dials to be used. I added grooves to the sides for
better friction during use and looked at the datasheet for the potentiometer to create the hole it will be inserted into. We should be ready to print these and
test them out, I exported these models in formats used by 3D printers so we should all have access to them for whenever one of us gets the opportunity to print.
As usual, an image of the latest design will be pushed with this journal.
